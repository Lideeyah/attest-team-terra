Open the register

If you run a carbon project

Register the project against its registry record, enrol households with a named agent, fund a period and close it. The commitment comes from your own project design document, quoted with its page number. What the register publishes is what the bank settled.

If you assess or buy from carbon projects

Follow a project and read its periods: households enrolled, households paid, what each period required and what it fell short by, confirmation rates, disputes and their resolution basis. Verify a period against its hash, or one transfer against its proof, without seeing anything else.
